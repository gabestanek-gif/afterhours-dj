Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version

Your music stays on your device. After Hours never uploads your songs, playlists or sets.

An account is optional. If you make one, we keep your email address so you can sign in.

Usage data is only sent if you allow it, and it never includes song titles, artists or lyrics.

We don’t sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.

What stays on your device

The music files you import, the analysis of each song (tempo, key, sections), separated stems, playlists, sets, settings and lyric files are stored only in the app on your device. Mixing and stem separation run on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What we collect

Account (optional): your email address, your password (stored hashed by our account provider; we never see it), whether you asked for feature emails, and when you signed in.

Usage data (only if you tap Allow): which app features you use, such as starting a set, the mixing style you pick, the kinds of transitions played and how many songs you added; the app version, device type and screen size; and error messages when something goes wrong. It’s linked to a random ID, or to your account ID if you’re signed in, never to your email address.

Support messages: what you choose to send us by email.

How we use it

To run your account (signing in, sign-in codes, password resets, account deletion), to see which features are used and fix problems, and to answer you when you contact us. We send feature emails only if you asked for them.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run these features for us: Supabase (accounts and sign-in emails) and PostHog (usage data, stored in the United States). We don’t sell or rent personal information, and we don’t share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Account information is kept until you delete your account. Usage data is kept for up to two years and then deleted. Support emails are kept while we’re helping you and for a reasonable time after.

Your choices

Turn usage data on or off any time under Account in the app. Delete your account any time in Settings › Delete My Account; this removes your account and email address from our account provider. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your information or to have it deleted.

Children

After Hours isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and passwords are stored hashed by our account provider. No method is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
