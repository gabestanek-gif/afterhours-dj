Support

We usually reply within two days.

Email afterhoursdj.help@gmail.com with what happened and what you expected. You can also write to us from the app: Settings › Contact Support.

Adding songs

Tap the download icon on the left of the bottom bar, then choose files, or tap Import a ZIP of songs to add a whole ZIP at once. MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC and OGG files work. Songs from streaming services can’t be added, because those services don’t allow their songs to be mixed by other apps.

No songs to try it with?

Download our sample songs (6 short original tracks made for testing, free to use). On iPhone, tap the link, then in After Hours tap Import a ZIP of songs and pick it from Downloads in the Files app.

Lyrics and wordplay

Wordplay transitions use lyrics that come with your songs: lyrics saved in a song’s tags, or a .lrc lyric file with the same name as the song, added along with it (or inside the same ZIP).

Deleting your account

Settings › Delete My Account. Your music stays on your device.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. Your music never leaves your device.
